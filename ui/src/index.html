GAME OVERLAY

Hidden

A small see-through panel that sits on top of your game and shows the readings you choose. Clicks pass through it to the game.

Hotkey Ctrl+Shift+O

It shows over games in windowed or borderless fullscreen mode. It cannot draw over a game in exclusive fullscreen. FPS comes from the game that has the focus, and shows only while the overlay is on.
